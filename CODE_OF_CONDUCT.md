# Code of Conduct

All contributors to KAVACH are expected to behave respectfully and professionally.

## Our Standards

- Be inclusive and welcoming.
- Be respectful in your language and actions.
- Focus on ideas, not individuals.
- Avoid harassment, discriminatory language, and abusive behavior.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by opening an issue or contacting the repository maintainers directly.
